Bin edges: this run's raw output only ships bin CENTERS (Diameter_um) plus

(unlike the Stage-3 data/psd/* copies). psd_measured.py reconstructs the n+1
edges from the n log-spaced centers by inverting psd_parametric's own
edge->center convention (center = sqrt(edge_lo*edge_hi)), which recovers the
edges essentially exactly for a genuinely log-spaced grid (verified: this
run's centers are log-spaced from ~2.23 to ~906.45 um in 32 bins, matching
its own summary.json "range" min/max exactly).
